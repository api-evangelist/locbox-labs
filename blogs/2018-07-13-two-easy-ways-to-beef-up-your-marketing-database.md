Fact: Your customer email marketing database degrades by about 25% each year. Your contact’s email addresses change, they may unsubscribe, or sometimes you just have contacts that use fake emails to fill out forms on various websites…yours included. Whatever the reason may be, it is crucial to your business’s marketing success that you pay attention to your database.
